Proportional representationसमानुपातिक प्रतिनिधित्व

Glossaryशब्दावली

Seats are shared between parties in proportion to their share of votes, using closed party lists in Nepal.मतको अनुपातअनुसार दलहरूबीच सिट बाँडिन्छ। नेपालमा बन्द सूची प्रयोग हुन्छ।

Key factsमुख्य तथ्यहरू

Seats in the House of Representativesप्रतिनिधि सभामा सिट110११०
Seats in provincial assembliesप्रदेश सभामा सिट220२२०

Under proportional representation, voters vote for a party list rather than a single candidate in that part of the election. Seats are then allocated in proportion to each party's share of the vote, subject to any threshold that applies.समानुपातिक प्रतिनिधित्वमा मतदाताले उम्मेदवारको सट्टा दलको सूचीमा मतदान गर्छन्। त्यसपछि मतको अनुपातअनुसार सिट बाँडिन्छ।

In Nepal, proportional representation provides 110 seats in the House of Representatives and 220 seats in provincial assemblies. Parties submit closed lists, and the Election Commission allocates seats from those lists after the vote. The exact threshold rules are covered in the electoral systems article, and any figure not verified there is marked To confirm.नेपालमा समानुपातिक प्रणालीबाट प्रतिनिधि सभामा ११० र प्रदेश सभामा २२० सिट भरिन्छन्।
