Mixed electoral systemमिश्रित निर्वाचन प्रणाली

Glossaryशब्दावली

A system that combines directly elected constituency seats with proportional list seats.प्रत्यक्ष निर्वाचन क्षेत्रका सिट र समानुपातिक सूचीका सिटलाई जोड्ने प्रणाली।

Key factsमुख्य तथ्यहरू

House of Representatives totalप्रतिनिधि सभा जम्मा275 (165 direct, 110 proportional)२७५ (१६५ प्रत्यक्ष, ११० समानुपातिक)
Provincial assemblies totalप्रदेश सभा जम्मा550 (330 direct, 220 proportional)५५० (३३० प्रत्यक्ष, २२० समानुपातिक)

A mixed system uses two ballots or two parts of one election. One part elects local members directly, and the other part tops up representation so that the overall result is closer to each party's share of the vote.मिश्रित प्रणालीमा एउटा भागबाट स्थानीय सदस्य प्रत्यक्ष निर्वाचित हुन्छन् र अर्को भागले मतको अनुपात मिलाउँछ।

Nepal's House of Representatives has 275 seats in total: 165 first past the post seats and 110 proportional seats. Provincial assemblies have 550 seats in total: 330 first past the post seats and 220 proportional seats. The Constituent Assembly elections of 2008 and 2013 used a similar mixed system, with 240 first past the post seats and 335 proportional seats out of 601 total seats.नेपालको प्रतिनिधि सभामा २७५ सिट छन्: १६५ प्रत्यक्ष र ११० समानुपातिक। प्रदेश सभामा ५५० सिट छन्: ३३० प्रत्यक्ष र २२० समानुपातिक।
