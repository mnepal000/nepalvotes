Turnoutमतदान प्रतिशत

Glossaryशब्दावली

The share of registered or eligible voters who cast a ballot.दर्ता भएका वा योग्य मतदातामध्ये मतदान गर्नेहरूको हिस्सा।

Turnout is usually reported as a percentage. It can be calculated against registered voters or against all eligible voters, and different sources sometimes use different bases, so small differences between reports are common.मतदान प्रतिशत सामान्यतया प्रतिशतमा देखाइन्छ। यो दर्ता भएका वा योग्य मतदाताको आधारमा निकाल्न सकिन्छ, त्यसैले स्रोतअनुसार सानो फरक पर्न सक्छ।

NepalVotes shows the turnout figure from the source read for each election, and notes where sources disagree, for example for 2013. Turnout alone does not show how many votes were valid, spoiled, or rejected.नेपालभोट्सले प्रत्येक निर्वाचनका लागि पढिएको स्रोतको मतदान प्रतिशत देखाउँछ।
