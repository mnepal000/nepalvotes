Delimitationनिर्वाचन क्षेत्र निर्धारण

Glossaryशब्दावली

The process of drawing constituency boundaries, usually based on population.सामान्यतया जनसंख्याका आधारमा निर्वाचन क्षेत्रको सीमा कोर्ने प्रक्रिया।

Key factsमुख्य तथ्यहरू

Federal constituencies setसंघीय क्षेत्र संख्या165१६५
Provincial constituencies setप्रदेश क्षेत्र संख्या330३३०

Delimitation divides a country into constituencies of broadly comparable population, so that each vote carries broadly similar weight. Boundaries are normally reviewed after a census or a constitutional change.निर्वाचन क्षेत्र निर्धारणले देशलाई करिब बराबर जनसंख्या भएका क्षेत्रमा बाँड्छ, ताकि प्रत्येक मतको भार करिब बराबर होस्।

Nepal's current 165 federal constituencies and 330 provincial constituencies were set through delimitation before the 2017 elections. The delimitation article covers the commission and criteria in detail, with unverified points marked To confirm.नेपालका हालका १६५ संघीय र ३३० प्रदेश निर्वाचन क्षेत्र २०१७ को निर्वाचनअघि निर्धारण गरिएका हुन्।
