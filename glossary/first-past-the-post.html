First past the postपहिलो हुने निर्वाचित हुने प्रणाली

Glossaryशब्दावली

The candidate with the most votes in a constituency wins the seat, even without a majority of votes.कुनै निर्वाचन क्षेत्रमा सबैभन्दा बढी मत पाउने उम्मेदवारले सिट जित्छन्, बहुमत नपाए पनि।

Key factsमुख्य तथ्यहरू

Seats in the House of Representativesप्रतिनिधि सभामा सिट165१६५
Seats in provincial assembliesप्रदेश सभामा सिट330३३०

In a first past the post election, each constituency elects one member. Voters choose one candidate, and the candidate with the highest number of valid votes wins. There is no second round and no redistribution of votes.पहिलो हुने निर्वाचित हुने प्रणालीमा प्रत्येक निर्वाचन क्षेत्रबाट एक जना सदस्य निर्वाचित हुन्छन्। सबैभन्दा बढी सदर मत पाउने उम्मेदवार विजयी हुन्छन्।

In Nepal, first past the post is used for 165 seats in the House of Representatives, 330 seats in provincial assemblies, and for directly elected local posts such as mayors and chairs. It is the direct part of Nepal's mixed system, alongside proportional representation.नेपालमा यो प्रणाली प्रतिनिधि सभाका १६५ सिट, प्रदेश सभाका ३३० सिट र मेयर तथा अध्यक्षजस्ता प्रत्यक्ष स्थानीय पदका लागि प्रयोग हुन्छ।
