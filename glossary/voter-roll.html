Voter rollमतदाता नामावली

Glossaryशब्दावली

The official list of people eligible to vote in an election.निर्वाचनमा मतदान गर्न योग्य व्यक्तिहरूको आधिकारिक सूची।

Key factsमुख्य तथ्यहरू

Prepared byतयार गर्ने निकायElection Commission of Nepalनिर्वाचन आयोग, नेपाल

A voter roll, also called an electoral roll, lists every registered voter for an area. Election officials use it to check identity at the polling centre and to prevent double voting.मतदाता नामावलीमा कुनै क्षेत्रका सबै दर्ता भएका मतदाताको नाम हुन्छ। मतदान केन्द्रमा पहिचान जाँच्न र दोहोरो मतदान रोक्न यसको प्रयोग हुन्छ।

In Nepal, the Election Commission prepares and updates the voter roll. Details of registration drives and the total number of registered voters vary by election, so this article does not state a single total. Election pages give the turnout figure where it was verified for that election.नेपालमा निर्वाचन आयोगले मतदाता नामावली तयार र अद्यावधिक गर्छ।
