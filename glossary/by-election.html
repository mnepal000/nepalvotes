By-electionउपनिर्वाचन

Glossaryशब्दावली

An election held between general elections to fill a vacant seat.सामान्य निर्वाचनबीच खाली सिट भर्न गरिने निर्वाचन।

Key factsमुख्य तथ्यहरू

Coverage in NepalVotesनेपालभोट्समा समावेशNot yet, To confirm from Election Commission sourcesअझै छैन, निर्वाचन आयोग स्रोतबाट पुष्टि गर्न बाँकी

A by-election is held when a seat becomes vacant during a term, for example after a resignation or death. Only voters in that constituency or area take part.कुनै सिट कार्यकालबीच खाली भएमा उपनिर्वाचन हुन्छ, जस्तै राजीनामा वा निधनपछि। त्यस क्षेत्रका मतदाताले मात्र मतदान गर्छन्।

By-election results are not yet included as separate pages in NepalVotes v1 or v2. They are listed as a gap in the README, and will need direct Election Commission sourcing before they are added.नेपालभोट्समा उपनिर्वाचन नतिजा अझै छुट्टै पृष्ठका रूपमा समावेश छैनन्।
