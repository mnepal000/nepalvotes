Inclusion quotaसमावेशी कोटा

Glossaryशब्दावली

Reserved places or list requirements that ensure representation of groups that are otherwise underrepresented.अन्यथा कम प्रतिनिधित्व हुने समूहको प्रतिनिधित्व सुनिश्चित गर्न आरक्षित स्थान वा सूची व्यवस्था।

Key factsमुख्य तथ्यहरू

Exact percentagesठ्याक्कै प्रतिशतTo confirm in the inclusion quotas articleसमावेशी कोटा लेखमा पुष्टि गर्न बाँकी

Inclusion quotas can take the form of reserved seats, or requirements that party lists include candidates from specified groups. Their exact form varies by country and by election.समावेशी कोटा आरक्षित सिट वा दलको सूचीमा निश्चित समूहका उम्मेदवार समावेश गर्नुपर्ने व्यवस्था हुन सक्छ।

In Nepal, proportional lists must reflect inclusion of women and other groups under the constitution and election law. The exact percentages and group categories are covered in the inclusion quotas article, and any figure not verified there is marked To confirm. This glossary entry does not state a percentage on its own.नेपालमा समानुपातिक सूचीले संविधान र निर्वाचन कानुनअनुसार महिला तथा अन्य समूहको समावेशिता देखाउनुपर्छ।
