The 1959 First Electionसन् १९५९ को पहिलो आम निर्वाचन

Eras and conceptsयुग र अवधारणाहरू

Nepal's first general election was held in February to April 1959 under a new constitution adopted days before voting began, and the Nepali Congress won a clear two thirds majority.नेपालमा पहिलो आम निर्वाचन सन् १९५९ मा भयो। नेपाली कांग्रेसले ठूलो बहुमत जित्यो र बी. पी. कोइराला प्रधानमन्त्री बने।

After the fall of the Rana regime in 1951, Nepal was ruled by short lived governments under an interim constitution while elections were repeatedly delayed. King Mahendra, who succeeded King Tribhuvan in 1955, presented a new constitution on 12 February 1959 and announced elections.पहिलो आम निर्वाचन सन् १९५९ मा नयाँ संविधानअन्तर्गत भयो।

Voting ran from 18 February to 3 April 1959 in the most commonly cited account, although one secondary source gives 11 March as the closing date, so the closing date is To confirm. This is the same discrepancy already listed in the v1 gaps, and the election page uses 18 February to 3 April 1959. Nine parties fielded candidates across 109 constituencies. The Nepali Congress won 74 seats with 666,898 votes, and the Nepal Rashtrabadi Gorkha Parishad was second with 19 seats. Bishweshwar Prasad (B. P.) Koirala became Nepal's first democratically elected prime minister.
