Federal Era After 2015सङ्घीय युग

Eras and conceptsयुग र अवधारणाहरू

The Constitution of 2015 created a federal democratic republic with three levels of government, and the first elections at all three levels were completed between May 2017 and March 2018.सन् २०१५ को संविधानले सङ्घ, प्रदेश र स्थानीय गरी तीन तहको सरकार बनायो। सन् २०१७ मा तीनै तहको पहिलो निर्वाचन भयो।

The Constitution promulgated on 20 September 2015 replaced the Interim Constitution of 2007 and the unitary state with a federal structure of one federal government, seven provincial governments, and 753 local governments across 77 districts. The Constitution required elections at all three levels before the term of the Legislature Parliament expired on 21 January 2018.सन् २०१५ को संविधानले सङ्घ, प्रदेश र स्थानीय गरी तीन तहको सरकार बनायो।

Local elections were held in three phases on 14 May, 28 June, and 18 September 2017. House of Representatives and provincial assembly elections were held together in two phases on 26 November and 7 December 2017. The National Assembly election followed on 7 February 2018, and the presidential election on 13 March 2018, completing the first full cycle under the new Constitution.सन् २०१७ मा तीनै तहको पहिलो निर्वाचन भयो र सन् २०१८ मा राष्ट्रिय सभा र राष्ट्रपति निर्वाचनले पहिलो चक्र पूरा गर्यो।

A second general election cycle for the House and provincial assemblies was held on 20 November 2022, with 17,988,570 registered voters reported for that election.
