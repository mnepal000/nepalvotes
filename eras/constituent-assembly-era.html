Constituent Assembly Era (2008 and 2013)संविधानसभा युग

Eras and conceptsयुग र अवधारणाहरू

Two Constituent Assemblies were elected to write a new constitution after the end of the armed conflict and the monarchy. The first, elected in 2008, was dissolved in 2012 without a constitution, and the second, elected in 2013, promulgated the Constitution in 2015.द्वन्द्व अन्त्यपछि नयाँ संविधान लेख्न दुई संविधानसभा निर्वाचित भए। पहिलो सभाले संविधान बनाउन सकेन, दोस्रो सभाले सन् २०१५ मा संविधान जारी गर्‍यो।

The decade long armed conflict ended with the Comprehensive Peace Agreement in November 2006 between the Government of Nepal and the Communist Party of Nepal (Maoist). A central pillar of the accord was a new constitution. The Interim Constitution of 2007, adopted 15 January 2007, set out democratic commitments and the path to a Constituent Assembly.द्वन्द्व अन्त्यपछि नयाँ संविधान लेख्न दुई संविधानसभा निर्वाचित भए।

The first Constituent Assembly election was held on 10 April 2008, and on 28 May 2008 the Assembly abolished the monarchy and declared Nepal a federal democratic republic. The Assembly also acted as the legislature. It was given an initial two year mandate to complete the constitution by May 2010, but the deadline was extended several times. Parties failed to agree on key issues, especially the number and boundaries of federal provinces and the form of government, and the Supreme Court ruled the Assembly could not continue indefinitely. The first Assembly was dissolved in May 2012 without promulgating a constitution.पहिलो सभाले संविधान बनाउन सकेन र सन् २०१२ मा विघटन भयो। दोस्रो सभाले सन् २०१५ मा संविधान जारी गर्यो।

A caretaker government oversaw the second Constituent Assembly election in November 2013. After the April and May 2015 earthquakes, major parties reached a 16 point agreement and fast tracked the draft, and the second Assembly adopted the Constitution on 20 September 2015.
