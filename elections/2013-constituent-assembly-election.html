Systemप्रणालीMixed: 240 first-past-the-post seats and 335 proportional seats, of 601 total seatsमिश्रित: २४० प्रत्यक्ष र ३३५ समानुपातिक सिट, कुल ६०१ सिटमध्ये

Sushil Koirala of the Nepali Congress became prime minister.नेपाली कांग्रेसका सुशील कोइराला प्रधानमन्त्री बने।

This was the second Constituent Assembly election, after the first assembly was dissolved in 2012 without a constitution. The Nepali Congress finished first, CPN (UML) second, and the Maoists fell sharply to third. This is the election the original NepalVotes site followed live in 2013.पहिलो संविधानसभा २०१२ मा संविधानबिना विघटन भएपछि भएको यो दोस्रो संविधानसभा निर्वाचन थियो। नेपाली कांग्रेस पहिलो, नेकपा (एमाले) दोस्रो र माओवादी तेस्रो भयो।
