The left alliance won a majority in six of the seven provinces. Province level chief ministers varied by province and are not listed in v1.वाम गठबन्धनले सातमध्ये छ प्रदेशमा बहुमत जित्यो। प्रदेशका मुख्यमन्त्री प्रदेशअनुसार फरक थिए र v1 मा सूचीबद्ध छैनन्।

These were Nepal's first provincial elections, held together with the 2017 general election. CPN (UML) finished first with 243 seats, followed by the Nepali Congress with 113 and the Maoist Centre with 108. Party vote totals by province are a v1 gap, so only seats are shown here.२०१७ को आम निर्वाचनसँगै भएका यी नेपालका पहिलो प्रदेश निर्वाचन थिए। नेकपा (एमाले) २४३ सिटसहित पहिलो भयो।
