Communist Party of Nepal (Unified Marxist-Leninist)नेपाल कम्युनिष्ट पार्टी (एकीकृत मार्क्सवादी-लेनिनवादी)

Partiesदलहरू

CPN (UML) is a Marxist-Leninist party formed in January 1991 from a merger, and one of Nepal's two largest parties since the restoration of multiparty democracy.नेकपा (एमाले) कम्युनिस्ट पार्टी हो। यो सन् १९९१ मा बनेको थियो। यसका अध्यक्ष केपी शर्मा ओली हुन्।

CPN (UML) was formed on 6 January 1991 through the unification of the Communist Party of Nepal (Marxist) and the Communist Party of Nepal (Marxist-Leninist). A single founder is To confirm, because sources describe a merger rather than a single founder. Man Mohan Adhikari was its first parliamentary leader.नेकपा (एमाले) सन् १९९१ मा दुई कम्युनिस्ट पार्टीको एकीकरणबाट बनेको थियो। यसले १९९४ पछि मनमोहन अधिकारीको नेतृत्वमा अल्पमत सरकार चलायो।

The party was the main opposition after the 1991 election and led a minority government under Man Mohan Adhikari after the 1994 election. It later led governments under Madhav Kumar Nepal and Jhala Nath Khanal during the first Constituent Assembly period, and under K. P. Sharma Oli after the new constitution and after the 2017 election.सन् २०१८ मा यो माओवादी केन्द्रसँग गाभिएर नेपाल कम्युनिस्ट पार्टी बन्यो, तर सन् २०२१ मा सर्वोच्च अदालतको फैसलापछि दुवै पुराना दल पुनःस्थापित भए।

On 17 May 2018, CPN (UML) merged with CPN (Maoist Centre) to form the Nepal Communist Party. On 8 March 2021, the Supreme Court ruled that the allocation of the name Nepal Communist Party to that merger was void from the start, because the name was already allotted to a party led by Rishiram Kattel, and the predecessor parties were revived.
