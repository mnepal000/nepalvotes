Rastriya Janata Party Nepal was formed in April 2017 by the merger of six Madhesi parties, and it merged in April 2020 into Janata Samajbadi Party, Nepal.राष्ट्रिय जनता पार्टी, नेपाल सन् २०१७ मा बनेको थियो। यो छ मधेशी पार्टीको एकीकरण थियो। पछि यो जनता समाजवादी पार्टीमा गाभियो।

Rastriya Janata Party Nepal was formed in April 2017 by the merger of six Madhesi parties, including Tarai Madhesh Loktantrik Party, Sadbhavana Party, and Madhesi Jana Adhikar Forum (Republican). The table gives 20 April 2017 and the article text gives 21 April 2017, so the exact founding day is To confirm.राष्ट्रिय जनता पार्टी, नेपाल सन् २०१७ मा छ मधेशी पार्टीको एकीकरणबाट बनेको थियो।

It merged on 22 April 2020 with Samajbadi Party, Nepal to form Janata Samajbadi Party, Nepal. It was led by a six member presidium including Mahantha Thakur.सन् २०२० मा यो जनता समाजवादी पार्टीमा गाभियो।
