Nagrik Unmukti Party, also People's Freedom Partyनागरिक उन्मुक्ति पार्टी

Partiesदलहरू

Nagrik Unmukti Party was formed in January 2022 under the coordination of Resham Lal Chaudhary, with its main base in the Tharuhath region, especially Kailali and Bardiya.नागरिक उन्मुक्ति पार्टी सन् २०२२ मा बनेको थियो। यसका संस्थापक रेशमलाल चौधरी हुन्। यसको आधार थारुहट क्षेत्र हो।

Nagrik Unmukti Party was formed on 3 January 2022 under the coordination of Resham Lal Chaudhary after he left People's Socialist Party, Nepal, with his wife Ranjeeta Shrestha chosen as chair because he could not chair the party under the law at that time. Its main base is in the Tharuhath region, especially Kailali and Bardiya.नागरिक उन्मुक्ति पार्टी सन् २०२२ मा रेशमलाल चौधरीको संयोजनमा बनेको थियो।

Later status is To confirm against the Election Commission. The older party is listed as merged into Nepali Communist Party, while a separate Nagrik Unmukti Party, Nepal was founded on 30 October 2025, chaired by Kabir Sob and launched by Resham Lal Chaudhary, with a common janto symbol alliance reported with Ashok Rai led People's Socialist Party and Rajendra Mahato led Rastriya Mukti Party Nepal.यसको पछिल्लो स्थिति निर्वाचन आयोगबाट पुष्टि गर्न बाँकी छ।
