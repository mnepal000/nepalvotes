Rastriya Prajatantra Partyराष्ट्रिय प्रजातन्त्र पार्टी

Partiesदलहरू

Rastriya Prajatantra Party is a constitutional monarchist and Hindu nationalist party formed in 1990 by Panchayat era prime ministers Surya Bahadur Thapa and Lokendra Bahadur Chand.राष्ट्रिय प्रजातन्त्र पार्टी हिन्दु राष्ट्र र राजसंस्थाको पक्षमा उभिने पार्टी हो। यो सन् १९९० मा बनेको थियो। यसका अध्यक्ष राजेन्द्र लिङ्देन हुन्। यसको चुनाव चिन्ह हलो हो।

Rastriya Prajatantra Party was formed on 29 May 1990 by Surya Bahadur Thapa and Lokendra Bahadur Chand. Thapa and Chand were also appointed prime minister by King Gyanendra in the 2000s, Chand in 2002 and Thapa in 2003.राष्ट्रिय प्रजातन्त्र पार्टी सन् १९९० मा सूर्यबहादुर थापा र लोकेन्द्रबहादुर चन्दबाट बनेको थियो।

The party went through repeated splits in the royalist camp. On 21 November 2016, Rastriya Prajatantra Party and Kamal Thapa led Rastriya Prajatantra Party Nepal announced unification under the name Rastriya Prajatantra Party. Prakash Chandra Lohani split away after the decision to join government and over the electoral symbol issue.सन् २०२१ को महाधिवेशनमा राजेन्द्र लिङ्देन अध्यक्ष निर्वाचित भए।

Rajendra Lingden was elected chairman at the party general convention in December 2021, defeating incumbent Kamal Thapa.
