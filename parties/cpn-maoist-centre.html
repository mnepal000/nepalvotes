Communist Party of Nepal (Maoist Centre)नेपाल कम्युनिस्ट पार्टी (माओवादी केन्द्र)

Partiesदलहरू

CPN (Maoist Centre) grew from the Maoist movement that launched an armed conflict in 1996, became the largest party in the 2008 Constituent Assembly election, and merged again in November 2025.माओवादी केन्द्र कम्युनिस्ट पार्टी हो। यसले सन् १९९६ मा सशस्त्र द्वन्द्व सुरु गरेको थियो। पछि यो शान्ति प्रक्रियामा आयो। यसका नेता पुष्पकमल दाहाल प्रचण्ड हुन्।

Sources list several dates for successive forms: 1994 as CPN (Unity Centre) in parallel, 1996 as CPN (Maoist), 2009 as Unified CPN (Maoist), 2016 as CPN (Maoist Centre), and 8 March 2021 as the second iteration after the Supreme Court revival. Founders named in one source read are Pushpa Kamal Dahal and Baburam Bhattarai. The party split from CPN (Unity Centre).माओवादी केन्द्र १९९६ को सशस्त्र द्वन्द्वबाट आएको पार्टी हो र २००८ को संविधानसभा निर्वाचनमा सबैभन्दा ठूलो दल बन्यो।

On 18 June 2012, vice chairman Mohan Baidya split and formed the Communist Party of Nepal-Maoist. A faction under Matrika Yadav also split from UCPN (Maoist) in 2009 in the sources read.सन् २०१८ मा यो एमालेसँग गाभियो, सन् २०२१ मा पुनःस्थापित भयो र सन् २०२५ मा फेरि नेपाली कम्युनिस्ट पार्टीमा गाभियो।

On 17 May 2018, CPN (UML) and CPN (Maoist Centre) dissolved into the Nepal Communist Party, which retained the UML sun symbol. On 8 March 2021, the Supreme Court ruled the merger void from the start on the name issue, and both predecessor parties were revived. In 2021, MPs including Prabhu Sah and Ram Bahadur Thapa left the Maoist Centre for CPN (UML), while Madhav Kumar Nepal and Jhala Nath Khanal left CPN (UML) to form CPN (Unified Socialist).On 17 May 2018, CPN (UML) and CPN (Maoist Centre) dissolved into the Nepal Communist Party, which retained the UML sun symbol. On 8 March 2021, the Supreme Court ruled the merger void from the start on the name issue, and both predecessor parties were revived. In 2021, MPs including Prabhu Sah and Ram Bahadur Thapa left the Maoist Centre for CPN (UML), while Madhav Kumar Nepal and Jhala Nath Khanal left CPN (UML) to form CPN (Unified Socialist).

On 5 November 2025, a unity conference at Bhrikutimandap declared the formation of the Nepali Communist Party through a merger of ten leftist parties and groups, including CPN (Maoist Centre) and CPN (Unified Socialist). Pushpa Kamal Dahal is listed as coordinator and Madhav Kumar Nepal as joint coordinator. Sources also list a 4 November 2025 dissolution date for the second iteration of CPN (Maoist Centre), and a minority group led by Janardan Sharma forming a separate campaign.
