Rastriya Janamorcha is the legal front of the Communist Party of Nepal (Masal) led by Mohan Bikram Singh, chaired by Chitra Bahadur K.C. It was originally founded in 1995 in one source, listed as 1999 original in another, and reestablished in 2006 after breaking from Janamorcha Nepal, so the founding year needs a single source decision before publication as a settled year.राष्ट्रिय जनमोर्चा नेकपा (मसाल) को कानुनी मोर्चा हो र यसका अध्यक्ष चित्रबहादुर केसी हुन्।

It merged in 2002 with Samyukta Janamorcha Nepal to form Janamorcha Nepal, then broke away in 2006 to reclaim the Rastriya Janamorcha name.यसको स्थापना वर्षबारे स्रोतहरूमा फरक भएकाले ठ्याक्कै वर्ष पुष्टि गर्न बाँकी छ।
