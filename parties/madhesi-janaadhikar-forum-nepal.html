Madhesi Jana Adhikar Forum, Nepal began as a forum for Madheshi rights led by Upendra Yadav and Jay Prakash Gupta. It became a political party after the 2007 Madhesh movement.मधेशी जनअधिकार फोरम मधेशी अधिकारका लागि बनेको मञ्च थियो र पछि पार्टी बन्यो।

It merged on 15 or 16 June 2015 into the Federal Socialist Forum, Nepal, with both dates appearing in sources read, so the exact merger day is To confirm. The exact Devanagari spelling from a source text is also To confirm.सन् २०१५ मा यो संघीय समाजवादी फोरममा गाभियो।
