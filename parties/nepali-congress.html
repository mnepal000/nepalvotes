Nepali Congressनेपाली कांग्रेस

Partiesदलहरू

Nepali Congress is a social democratic party and one of Nepal's oldest major parties, formed in 1950 and the winner of Nepal's first general election in 1959.नेपाली कांग्रेस नेपालको पुरानो र ठूलो लोकतान्त्रिक पार्टी हो। यो पार्टी सन् १९५० मा बनेको थियो। यसले सामाजिक लोकतन्त्रमा विश्वास गर्छ।

The Nepali National Congress was founded by B. P. Koirala in Calcutta on 25 January 1947, and the Nepal Democratic Congress was founded by Subarna Shamsher Rana in Calcutta on 4 August 1948. The two merged in April 1950 to form Nepali Congress. Sources read give both 9 April and 10 April for the merger date, so the exact day is To confirm.नेपाली कांग्रेस सन् १९५० मा बनेको थियो र सन् १९५९ को पहिलो आम निर्वाचन जितेको थियो। यसका पहिलो निर्वाचित प्रधानमन्त्री बीपी कोइराला थिए।

After the fall of the Rana regime, Nepali Congress led Nepal's first elected government under B. P. Koirala after the 1959 election. After King Mahendra's 1960 coup, its leaders were imprisoned or exiled. The party returned as a major force after the 1990 Jana Andolan, and won the 1991, 1999, 2013, and 2022 national elections on seats, among others shown in its election record.हालको नेतृत्व विवादित छ। स्रोतहरूले गगन थापालाई निर्वाचन आयोगले मान्यता दिएको अध्यक्षका रूपमा देखाउँछन्, तर शेरबहादुर देउवा पक्षले यो प्रक्रिया विवादित बताएको छ।

Current leadership is disputed in sources read. Sources list Gagan Thapa as president and report that the Election Commission recognised the Thapa led faction in January 2026, awarding it the party name and the tree symbol after a special general convention. The Sher Bahadur Deuba faction disputes that process, and later 2026 reporting describes a parallel convention plan and a Supreme Court review still pending.
