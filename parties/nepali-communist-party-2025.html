Nepali Communist Party (2025 merger)नेपाली कम्युनिस्ट पार्टी (२०२५ एकीकरण)

Partiesदलहरू

Nepali Communist Party was declared in November 2025 through a merger of ten leftist parties and groups, including CPN (Maoist Centre) and CPN (Unified Socialist).नेपाली कम्युनिस्ट पार्टी सन् २०२५ मा दश वाम पार्टी र समूहको एकीकरणबाट घोषणा गरिएको थियो।

On 5 November 2025, a unity conference at Bhrikutimandap declared the formation of the Nepali Communist Party. Pushpa Kamal Dahal is listed as coordinator and Madhav Kumar Nepal as joint coordinator. Sources list a 4 November 2025 dissolution date for the second iteration of CPN (Maoist Centre).नेपाली कम्युनिस्ट पार्टी सन् २०२५ को नोभेम्बरमा घोषणा गरिएको थियो। यसका संयोजक पुष्पकमल दाहाल हुन्।
