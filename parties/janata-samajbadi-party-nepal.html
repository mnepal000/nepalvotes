People's Socialist Party, Nepal was formed in April 2020 by the merger of Samajbadi Party, Nepal and Rastriya Janata Party Nepal, and it is rooted in Madhesh and identity based politics.जनता समाजवादी पार्टी, नेपाल मधेश केन्द्रित पार्टी हो। यो सन् २०२० मा बनेको थियो। यसका अध्यक्ष उपेन्द्र यादव हुन्।

On 23 April 2020, Samajbadi Party, Nepal and Rastriya Janata Party Nepal merged to form Janata Samajbadi Party, Nepal. The merger application was handed to the Election Commission on that date. The new party had a combined strength of 34 members in the House of Representatives at formation in one contemporary report. In May to July 2021, a dispute between Upendra Yadav and Mahantha Thakur ended with the Election Commission handing control to the Yadav faction. The Thakur faction then formed Loktantrik Samajwadi Party, Nepal. Resham Lal Chaudhary also left to form a new party in the same period, later known as Nagrik Unmukti Party.सन् २०२१ र २०२४ मा यसबाट छुट्टिएर नयाँ दलहरू बने।

On 5 May 2024, a group led by federal council chairman Ashok Rai applied to split, and the Election Commission recognised the new People's Socialist Party on 6 May 2024.
