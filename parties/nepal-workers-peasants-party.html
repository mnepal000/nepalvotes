Nepal Workers and Peasants Party is a Marxist-Leninist party founded on 23 January 1975 by Narayan Man Bijukchhe, with its main base in Bhaktapur. It began as the Nepal Workers and Peasants Organization and took its current party name before the 1991 election period in the sources read.नेपाल मजदुर किसान पार्टी सन् १९७५ मा नारायणमान बिजुक्छेबाट बनेको थियो।

It split in 1981 into Bijukchhe led and Hareram Sharma led groups, with the Bijukchhe group becoming the current party.यसको मुख्य आधार भक्तपुर हो।
