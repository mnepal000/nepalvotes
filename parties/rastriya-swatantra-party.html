Rastriya Swatantra Party is a centrist party founded in 2022 by former television journalist Rabi Lamichhane, which entered parliament in its first election in November 2022.राष्ट्रिय स्वतन्त्र पार्टी नयाँ पार्टी हो। यो सन् २०२२ मा बनेको थियो। यसका संस्थापक रवि लामिछाने हुन्। यसको चुनाव चिन्ह घण्टी हो।

On 16 June 2022, Rabi Lamichhane resigned as managing director of Galaxy 4K TV and announced he would contest the 2022 general election. On 21 June 2022, he announced the formation of Rastriya Swatantra Party with a 21 member central committee. The party was formally registered with the Election Commission on 1 July 2022, with Lamichhane as founding president.राष्ट्रिय स्वतन्त्र पार्टी सन् २०२२ मा रवि लामिछानेबाट बनेको थियो र सोही वर्षको निर्वाचनमा संसदमा प्रवेश गर्यो।

In the November 2022 general election, the party won 7 directly elected seats and, with about 10.7 percent of proportional representation votes in one source, became the fourth largest party at that time.
