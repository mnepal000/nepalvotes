Communist Party of Nepal (Unified Socialist)नेपाल कम्युनिष्ट पार्टी (एकीकृत समाजवादी)

Partiesदलहरू

CPN (Unified Socialist) was formed in August 2021 by Madhav Kumar Nepal and Jhala Nath Khanal after a dispute with CPN (UML), and it dissolved in November 2025 into the Nepali Communist Party merger.नेकपा (एकीकृत समाजवादी) सन् २०२१ मा बनेको थियो। यसका अध्यक्ष माधवकुमार नेपाल हुन्। पछि यो नेपाली कम्युनिस्ट पार्टीमा गाभियो।

Dissolvedविघटन4 November 2025 into Nepali Communist Party४ नोभेम्बर २०२५ मा नेपाली कम्युनिस्ट पार्टीमा गाभियो

On 18 August 2021, the President issued a second amendment to the political parties act relaxing split requirements to 20 percent or more of the parliamentary party and central committee. The Madhav Kumar Nepal faction then applied to register, initially under a name rendered as CPN-UML (Socialist) in one contemporary report, and the party was registered on 25 August 2021 after verification.नेकपा (एकीकृत समाजवादी) सन् २०२१ मा एमालेबाट छुट्टिएर बनेको थियो।

The party was a split from CPN (UML). Founders listed are Madhav Kumar Nepal, Jhala Nath Khanal, Pradeep Nepal, and others. Madhav Kumar Nepal remained chairman, and Jhala Nath Khanal served as senior leader in the sources read.सन् २०२५ मा यो नेपाली कम्युनिस्ट पार्टीमा गाभियो।

On 8 October 2021, CPN (Unified Socialist) and CPN (Unified) agreed to unite under the name CPN (Unified Socialist), with the pen as election symbol in that report. An earlier August 2021 application report lists an open book as the requested symbol. The final pre-merger symbol is therefore given as pen, with open book noted as the initial application symbol, and both should be checked against the Election Commission record if a single symbol is required. The party dissolved on 4 November 2025 into the Nepali Communist Party merger, with a small dissenting group reported as forming a reorganisation campaign, whose final status is To confirm.On 8 October 2021, CPN (Unified Socialist) and CPN (Unified) agreed to unite under the name CPN (Unified Socialist), with the pen as election symbol in that report.
