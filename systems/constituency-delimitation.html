Constituency Delimitationनिर्वाचन क्षेत्र निर्धारण

Systems and institutionsप्रणाली र संस्थाहरू

Delimitation is the drawing of electoral constituency boundaries for direct elections. In 2017, a dedicated commission redrew Nepal's boundaries for the new federal structure, setting 165 federal and 330 provincial constituencies.निर्वाचन क्षेत्र निर्धारण भनेको निर्वाचन क्षेत्रको सीमा तोक्ने काम हो। सन् २०१७ मा नयाँ सङ्घीय संरचनाका लागि १६५ सङ्घीय र ३३० प्रदेश निर्वाचन क्षेत्र तोकिए।

Before federalism, the House had 240 constituencies under the Constituent Assembly system. The 2015 Constitution required new boundaries for a 165 seat directly elected House and for provincial assemblies. The Government formed the Election Constituency Delimitation Commission in July 2017, with a short mandate.निर्वाचन क्षेत्र निर्धारणले प्रत्यक्ष निर्वाचनका लागि क्षेत्रको सीमा तोक्छ।

The commission submitted its report on 31 August 2017, as reported by IFES and contemporary reporting. One secondary source says 30 August 2017, so the exact report day is To confirm. Formation date is also To confirm: IFES reports 19 July 2017 and International IDEA reports 20 July 2017. The commission was chaired by Kamal Narayan Das, as reported in contemporary coverage.सन् २०१७ मा आयोगले १६५ संघीय र ३३० प्रदेश निर्वाचन क्षेत्र तोक्यो। प्रत्येक संघीय क्षेत्रलाई दुई प्रदेश क्षेत्रमा बाँडियो।

Each federal constituency was divided into two provincial constituencies. Criteria were population as the primary basis and geography as the secondary basis, with other considerations including transportation, access to government services, social diversity, administrative boundaries, and communities of interest. Weighting reported is 90 percent population and 10 percent geography. There was at least one electoral constituency in each district. Boundaries were to remain in force for 20 years and could not be challenged in court, under the constitutional provision cited by IFES and International IDEA.Each federal constituency was divided into two provincial constituencies.
