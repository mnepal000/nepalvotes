Presidential Election Systemराष्ट्रपति निर्वाचन प्रणाली

Systems and institutionsप्रणाली र संस्थाहरू

Nepal's President is not directly elected by voters but is chosen by an electoral college of federal and provincial lawmakers, with weighted votes, for a five year term renewable once.नेपालको राष्ट्रपति जनताबाट प्रत्यक्ष निर्वाचित हुँदैनन्। सङ्घीय र प्रदेश सांसदहरूले निर्वाचन मण्डलबाट राष्ट्रपति छान्छन्।

The presidency was created in the republican transition. After the 2008 Constituent Assembly abolished the monarchy, Nepal's head of state became a president. The 2015 Constitution, Part 6, Articles 62 to 64, sets out the election, term, and qualifications, as cited in constitutional summaries. Article numbering is To confirm against the official Constitution text. The Election Commission conducts the election. The President's role is largely ceremonial, with the Prime Minister as head of government, but the Constitution gives the President defined discretionary and formal powers.राष्ट्रपति प्रत्यक्ष निर्वाचनबाट होइन, निर्वाचक मण्डलबाट छानिन्छन्।

The electoral college is made up of voting members of the Federal Parliament (House of Representatives plus National Assembly) and members of the seven provincial assemblies. College size reported for the 2023 election context is 882 voters, comprising 332 federal parliament members and 550 provincial assembly members. Exact college size for the most recent election is To confirm, since one 2018 report gives 880 voters with 549 provincial members present at that time.राष्ट्रपतिको कार्यकाल पाँच वर्षको हुन्छ र एक पटक पुनः निर्वाचित हुन सकिन्छ।

Federal and provincial votes carry different weights, set by law. The formula reported is: total national population divided by total provincial assembly seats, rounded up, gives the provincial member weight; that weight multiplied by 1.64 and rounded up gives the federal member weight. Weights used in 2018 and 2023 were 48 for each provincial assembly member and 79 for each federal parliament member.Federal and provincial votes carry different weights, set by law. Winning rule: the candidate who receives a majority of votes of the total existing members of the electoral college is elected. If no candidate wins a majority, a second round is held between the two highest vote getters, requiring more than 50 percent. If neither passes 50 percent in the second round, a re-vote is held and the candidate with the most votes cast wins. Term is five years from election, renewable once, for a maximum of two terms.
