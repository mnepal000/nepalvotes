Voter Registration in Nepalमतदाता नामावली दर्ता

Systems and institutionsप्रणाली र संस्थाहरू

Only Nepali citizens whose names are on the electoral roll can vote, and voting age is 18. Registration is run by the Election Commission, with proof of citizenship and age, plus a photograph and fingerprints collected during enrolment.नेपालमा मतदान गर्न मतदाता नामावलीमा नाम हुनुपर्छ। मतदान गर्न १८ वर्ष पुगेको हुनुपर्छ।

Voter registration in Nepal is described as a continuous process, with periodic drives before elections. Citizens aged 16 or above who hold a citizenship certificate can register for the roll, but they cannot cast a ballot until they reach 18. For a specific election, the Election Commission sets a cutoff date. For the March 2026 House election, reporting said only voters turning 18 by 4 March 2026 and listed on the roll were eligible.नेपालमा मतदान गर्न १८ वर्ष पुगेको र नामावलीमा नाम भएको नेपाली नागरिक हुनुपर्छ।

Registration has traditionally required an in person visit to a district or provincial election office for biometric capture, after online pre-registration. In November 2025, reporting described a new option for National Identity Card holders to register online with biometric verification through the national ID database. Current online registration steps, deadlines, and National Identity Card linkage are To confirm against the Election Commission website, since that route was reported as newly launched in November 2025.१६ वर्ष पुगेका नागरिकता भएका व्यक्तिले नामावलीमा दर्ता गर्न सक्छन्, तर १८ वर्ष नपुगेसम्म मतदान गर्न सक्दैनन्।

Once on the roll, a voter does not need to re-register for the next election, subject to roll updates and corrections. Voters receive a voter identity card through the registration process.
