Election Commission of Nepalनेपालको निर्वाचन आयोग

Systems and institutionsप्रणाली र संस्थाहरू

The Election Commission of Nepal is the constitutional body that conducts, supervises, directs, and controls elections in Nepal, and it prepares the electoral rolls.नेपालको निर्वाचन आयोगले नेपालमा निर्वाचन गराउँछ। यसले राष्ट्रपति, सङ्घीय संसद, प्रदेश सभा र स्थानीय तहको निर्वाचनको व्यवस्थापन गर्छ।

The Commission traces its origin to the period after the 1950 revolution, and one source dates its formation to 12 November 1951. It has continued under later constitutions, including the Interim Constitution of 2007 and the Constitution of Nepal promulgated on 20 September 2015. Under Part 24 of the 2015 Constitution, Articles 245 to 247 cover its formation, functions, duties, and powers.निर्वाचन आयोग संवैधानिक निकाय हो र यसले नेपालका सबै निर्वाचनको व्यवस्थापन गर्छ।

The Commission is described in recent observer reporting as a constitutional body, independent in its election role, with a large temporary workforce drawn largely from civil servants during elections. Its main duties are to conduct, supervise, direct, and control elections for the President, Vice President, members of the Federal Parliament, members of provincial assemblies, and members of local levels, to prepare electoral rolls, and to hold a referendum on a matter of national importance where provided by the Constitution.आयोगमा प्रमुख निर्वाचन आयुक्तसहित पाँच आयुक्त हुन्छन्। आयुक्तको कार्यकालबारे स्रोतहरूमा फरक भएकाले ठ्याक्कै अवधि पुष्टि गर्न बाँकी छ।

The Commission has one Chief Election Commissioner and four other commissioners, five members in total, appointed by the President on the recommendation of the Constitutional Council. Reported qualifications are a bachelor's degree from a recognized university, not being a member of a political party at appointment, age 45 or above, and high moral character.
