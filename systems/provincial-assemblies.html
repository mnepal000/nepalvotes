Provincial Assembliesप्रदेश सभा

Systems and institutionsप्रणाली र संस्थाहरू

Nepal has seven unicameral provincial assemblies with 550 seats in total, each combining directly elected members and proportional representation members.नेपालमा सात प्रदेश सभा छन्। सबै प्रदेश सभामा जम्मा ५५० सदस्य हुन्छन् र उनीहरूको कार्यकाल पाँच वर्षको हुन्छ।

Provincial assemblies are new institutions of the federal era. They did not exist under the old unitary system. The first provincial assembly elections were held together with the federal election in two phases on 26 November and 7 December 2017.नेपालमा सात प्रदेश सभा छन् र जम्मा ५५० सिट छन्। पहिलो प्रदेश निर्वाचन सन् २०१७ मा संघीय निर्वाचनसँगै भएको थियो।

Each province's name and capital were to be chosen by its own provincial assembly by a two thirds majority vote, under Articles 295(2) and 288(2) respectively, as reported. Members serve five year terms, unless an assembly is dissolved earlier.प्रत्येक प्रदेशको नाम र राजधानी सम्बन्धित प्रदेश सभाले दुई तिहाइ बहुमतबाट छान्नुपर्ने व्यवस्था थियो।
