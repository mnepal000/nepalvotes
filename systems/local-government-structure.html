Local Government Structureस्थानीय तहको संरचना

Systems and institutionsप्रणाली र संस्थाहरू

Local government is the third level of government in Nepal, with 753 local units, each with its own elected executive and assembly, divided into wards.स्थानीय सरकार नेपालको तेस्रो तहको सरकार हो। नेपालमा ७५३ स्थानीय तह छन् र हरेक तहमा निर्वाचित जनप्रतिनिधि हुन्छन्।

The 2015 Constitution, Article 56, defines the three level structure of federation, province, and local level, and Schedule 8 gives local levels 22 powers. The old municipalities and village units, numbering more than 3,900, were restructured into 753 new local units on the recommendation of the Local Level Restructuring Commission.स्थानीय तह नेपालको तेस्रो तहको सरकार हो र ७५३ स्थानीय तह छन्।

Local elections under the new Constitution were first held in three phases in May, June, and September 2017, and the second set was held on 13 May 2022. Local units are: 6 metropolitan cities, 11 sub-metropolitan cities, 276 municipalities, and 460 rural municipalities. Those four numbers add to 753, verified against multiple sources.नगरपालिकामा मेयर र उपमेयर, गाउँपालिकामा अध्यक्ष र उपाध्यक्ष निर्वाचित हुन्छन्। प्रत्येक वडामा वडा अध्यक्ष र चार वडा सदस्य हुन्छन्।

Municipalities and metropolitan and sub-metropolitan cities elect a mayor and deputy mayor. Rural municipalities elect a chairperson and vice chairperson. Each ward elects a ward chairperson and four ward members. Of the four members, two must be women, and one of those two must be a Dalit woman, as reported in local election guides.
