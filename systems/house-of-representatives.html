House of Representativesप्रतिनिधि सभा

Systems and institutionsप्रणाली र संस्थाहरू

The House of Representatives is the lower house of Nepal's Federal Parliament, with 275 members elected for five years under a mixed system.प्रतिनिधि सभा नेपालको सङ्घीय संसदको तल्लो सदन हो। यसमा २७५ सदस्य हुन्छन् र उनीहरूको कार्यकाल पाँच वर्षको हुन्छ।

The current House was created under the Constitution of Nepal of 2015, which replaced the earlier unitary parliamentary structure with a federal parliament. Voters cast separate ballots for the direct constituency vote and the party vote for proportional seats.प्रतिनिधि सभा सन् २०१५ को संविधानअन्तर्गत बनेको थियो। यसमा १६५ प्रत्यक्ष र ११० समानुपातिक गरी २७५ सदस्य हुन्छन्।

The House uses a mixed parallel system: 165 members elected by first past the post from single member geographical constituencies, and 110 members elected by proportional representation, with the whole country treated as a single constituency for the party vote.प्रधानमन्त्री प्रतिनिधि सभाका सदस्य हुनुपर्छ र सदनको विश्वास पाउन सक्नुपर्छ। बहुमतका लागि १३८ सदस्य चाहिन्छ।

The House is the main house for forming the government. The Prime Minister must be a member of the House and must be able to command its confidence. The Prime Minister is not directly elected by voters, and is normally the leader who can command a majority, either as head of the largest party or as a coalition candidate, appointed by the President, who must then secure a vote of confidence in the House.
