Glossaryशब्दावली

First past the postपहिलो हुने निर्वाचित हुने प्रणाली

The candidate with the most votes in a constituency wins the seat, even without a majority of votes.कुनै निर्वाचन क्षेत्रमा सबैभन्दा बढी मत पाउने उम्मेदवारले सिट जित्छन्, बहुमत नपाए पनि।
Glossaryशब्दावली

Proportional representationसमानुपातिक प्रतिनिधित्व

Seats are shared between parties in proportion to their share of votes, using closed party lists in Nepal.मतको अनुपातअनुसार दलहरूबीच सिट बाँडिन्छ। नेपालमा बन्द सूची प्रयोग हुन्छ।
Glossaryशब्दावली

Mixed electoral systemमिश्रित निर्वाचन प्रणाली

A system that combines directly elected constituency seats with proportional list seats.प्रत्यक्ष निर्वाचन क्षेत्रका सिट र समानुपातिक सूचीका सिटलाई जोड्ने प्रणाली।
Glossaryशब्दावली

Voter rollमतदाता नामावली

The official list of people eligible to vote in an election.निर्वाचनमा मतदान गर्न योग्य व्यक्तिहरूको आधिकारिक सूची।
Glossaryशब्दावली

Constituencyनिर्वाचन क्षेत्र

A defined area that elects one or more members to a legislature.विधायिकामा एक वा बढी सदस्य निर्वाचित गर्ने निश्चित क्षेत्र।
Glossaryशब्दावली

Turnoutमतदान प्रतिशत

The share of registered or eligible voters who cast a ballot.दर्ता भएका वा योग्य मतदातामध्ये मतदान गर्नेहरूको हिस्सा।

Sources and method

Main sources used for v1 are the Election Commission of Nepal as reported in the sources below, IPU Parline, and election result tables that cite those sources. For mixed elections, votes and vote share are the proportional representation votes, as reported in the source tables. Party lists are top parties, not every party that won a seat, except where a total is stated.

Each election page also lists its own sources.प्रत्येक निर्वाचन पृष्ठमा त्यसका आफ्नै स्रोतहरू पनि दिइएका छन्।
