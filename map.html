Constituency map of Nepalनेपालको निर्वाचन क्षेत्र नक्सा

All 165 federal constituencies of the House of Representatives, from the Constituency Delimitation Commission report of 31 August 2017, grouped by province and district.३१ अगस्ट २०१७ को निर्वाचन क्षेत्र निर्धारण आयोगको प्रतिवेदन अनुसार प्रतिनिधि सभाका सबै १६५ सङ्घीय निर्वाचन क्षेत्रहरू, प्रदेश र जिल्ला अनुसार।

Please read this before using the mapनक्सा प्रयोग गर्नुअघि यो पढ्नुहोस्

This map shows district boundaries, NOT exact constituency polygons. No verified open file of all 165 constituency polygons was found for this build, so constituency boundaries inside a district are not drawn and are not guessed. Click a district to see its constituencies, then click a constituency to highlight its district and see its details. Every constituency is also in the list below, so the map is never the only way to reach one.यो नक्साले जिल्ला सीमाहरू देखाउँछ, निर्वाचन क्षेत्रका ठ्याक्कै सीमाहरू होइन। सबै १६५ निर्वाचन क्षेत्रका प्रमाणित खुला फाइल भेटिएन, त्यसैले जिल्लाभित्रका निर्वाचन क्षेत्र सीमाहरू कोरिएका छैनन् र अनुमान गरिएका छैनन्। जिल्लामा क्लिक गरेर त्यसका निर्वाचन क्षेत्रहरू हेर्नुहोस्, त्यसपछि निर्वाचन क्षेत्रमा क्लिक गरेर त्यसको जिल्ला र विवरण हेर्नुहोस्।

Sources and methodस्रोत र विधि

  • List of parliamentary constituencies of Nepal, based on the Constituency Delimitation Commission report of 31 August 2017, with 2022 electorate figures from the Election Commission of Nepal final roster as cited there. Province totals: Koshi 28, Madhesh 32, Bagmati 33, Gandaki 18, Lumbini 26, Karnali 12, Sudurpashchim 16, total 165.
  • District boundaries: Acesmndr nepal-geojson (MIT licence, 77 district polygons, coordinates rounded to 4 decimal places, no boundary shape changed in any other way). District names were matched to the constituency list, including Dhanusa to Dhanusha, Kavrepalanchowk to Kavrepalanchok, and Parasi to Nawalparasi (Bardaghat Susta West).
  • Base map tiles: OpenStreetMap contributors, via Leaflet, no API key. Attribution is also shown on the map itself.
  • What this page does not show: exact constituency polygons, constituency winners, candidates, or vote totals. Those are To confirm until a verified constituency polygon file and constituency result source are added. National results are on the 2026 general election and 2022 general election pages.

Note on names: constituency and district names are shown in English as in the delimitation list. Nawalpur is the Gandaki district formed from the eastern part of the old Nawalparasi district, and Nawalparasi (Bardaghat Susta West), also called Parasi, is the Lumbini district formed from its western part.नामसम्बन्धी टिप्पणी: निर्वाचन क्षेत्र र जिल्लाका नामहरू निर्धारण सूची अनुसार अङ्ग्रेजीमा देखाइएका छन्।
